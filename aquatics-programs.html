Integrated Therapy

Aquatic physical therapy classes. Integrated Therapy doesn't publish its Oakhurst class times; see aqua-pt.com. When its sessions are entered in the pool's schedule they appear on the Oakhurst calendar.

Decatur Gators

The city's summer swim team, ages 5–18, with home meets at Ebster Pool. Registration and practice times come out each spring.
