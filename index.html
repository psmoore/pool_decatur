Aquatics

The City of Decatur has three outdoor pools, Ebster, Glenlake and McKoy, and one indoor pool, Oakhurst, which is open year-round. Every pool's schedule on these pages is live: when the city changes it, these pages change with it.

Safety and weather

No large floats or tubes that block the lifeguards' view, and no water guns. Children in approved life jackets need close supervision; swimmers in diapers need swim diapers. Pools close during thunder and lightning: the two-hour limit is suspended while you wait, you can leave and come back later that day, and anyone who paid but couldn't get in the water gets a rain check.

Rentals

Pool parties, lane rentals and group visits (10 or more get $3 resident / $5 non-resident entry) are booked by form, at least two weeks ahead with payment in full. Call 404-377-0494.
