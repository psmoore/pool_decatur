Ebster Pool

A summer pool behind the Police Department, at Atlanta Ave. and West Trinity Place: 8 lanes, 25 yards, a big deck, a snack area and a shade shelter. Home pool of the Decatur Gators swim team, with morning swim lessons and lanes for lap swimmers.
